RISRC – Club Group Field Session Request

Rhode Island State Referee Committee

This form allows soccer clubs to submit a group request for referees who need to complete their required on-field training session. Please provide your club information and add all participating referees before submitting your request.

Club information

Fields marked * are required.

Referees

A minimum of 5 referees is required per group field session request. Add every referee who needs an on-field session. Referees under 18 require parent/guardian contact details.

Privacy and guardian consent

The information submitted is used only by the Rhode Island State Referee Committee to schedule and administer field sessions. It is stored in a private, access-restricted system and is not sold or shared with third parties. For referees under 18, the parent or guardian listed is the primary contact.
